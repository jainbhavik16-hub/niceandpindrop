Our Understanding of Your Business

Our pre-kickoff research into NiCE and Pindrop, from public sources — to align on what we have right and where you'll sharpen us.

NiCE · nice.com
CXone: The Enterprise CX AI Platform
An enterprise customer experience AI platform that orchestrates human and AI agents across voice and digital channels, with Cognigy providing the agentic AI and IVR modernization path. NiCE reports 25B+ interactions a year and 11 consecutive years as a Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader for CCaaS. Its group also includes NiCE Actimize for financial crime.
Pindrop · pindrop.com
Identity Trust for Real-Time Communications
A platform built on three engines: Synthetic (Pulse and BotStopper, to spot deepfakes and AI agents), Risk (Protect, for fraud) and Authentication (Passport and VeriCall, for passive caller authentication). Pindrop states it is trusted by 14 of the top 20 banks and 5 of the top 7 life insurers.
The NiCE + Pindrop Opportunity
How it fits together
  • ✓ On customer request, CXone routes a copy of the inbound call to Pindrop for real-time voice, device and risk analysis
  • ✓ Pindrop's agent interface embeds in CXone, so agents see authentication and fraud signals in their own workflow
  • ✓ A resale partnership lets customers buy Pindrop on NiCE paper within their CXone contract, extending to Cognigy AI agents

NiCE brings the CXone platform and Pindrop brings native voice authentication, fraud and deepfake detection — and Vieu maps the warm paths into UK financial institutions that turn that reach into booked meetings.

The offer is compelling — the challenge is go-to-market. Large financial institutions clear long, multi-stakeholder committees across the contact centre, fraud, information security and technology. The way through is warm access to the right leaders.
